**Organization:** I am the Co-Organizer of the [CREST 2026 Workshop](https://sites.google.com/view/crest26) (co-located with FLoC/CAV 2026). I was the Publicity Co-Chair for [CAV 2024](http://i-cav.org/2024/organization/) and was the Publicity Chair for [CAV 2021](http://i-cav.org/2021/organization/) (follow [@ConfCAV](https://twitter.com/confCAV) on Twitter/X!). I organized the [Buddy Program](http://i-cav.org/2019/mentoring/) at CAV 2019. 

**Speaking:** I participated in the *[Scientific Interaction](https://www.heidelberg-laureate-forum.org/forum/8th-hlf-2021/program-8th-hlf-2021.html#day-67-3)* at the [8th Heidelberg Laureate Forum](https://www.heidelberg-laureate-forum.org/forum/8th-hlf-2021.html) as a panelist on the topic of "Computer Science Career Paths in Academia" alongside Turing Award winner Barbara Liskov.

**Committees:** I was a Program Committee member of [CREST 2023](https://crest-etaps.github.io). I was on the Artifact Evaluation Committees of [VMCAI 2022](https://popl22.sigplan.org/home/VMCAI-2022#Call-for-Artifacts) and [CAV 2020](http://i-cav.org/2020/organization/).

**Journal Reviewing:** I reviewed for the Springer Journal [Formal Methods in System Design](https://www.springer.com/journal/10703). 

**Sub-Reviewing:** I sub-reviewed for the following scientific venues: DATE 2024, ATVA 2023, CAV 2022, LICS 2022, CONCUR 2021, CAV 2021, TACAS 2021, RV 2020, CONCUR 2020, ATVA 2020, CSL 2020, iFM 2019, ATVA 2019, SI-SR 2017, and SR 2017.

**Student Volunteer:** I was a student volunteer at FLoC 2018. 

**CAVlinks:** I started and maintain the [CAVlinks Website](http://i-cav.org/cavlinks/) together with a few other volunteers. CAVlinks is a sharing platform for the Computer-Aided Verification community.
